# Failures, limitations, and audit separation

1. The first terminal negative test's market stock 10,000 did not create a floor-price market. It had positive terminal profit (+44) and the expected rejection assertion failed. Exact first source, executable receipt, compile/run stdout/stderr are retained under `logs/failures/floor_fixture_v1/`. The final test uses genuine saturated stock 1,000,000 and zero profit; it passes. Production was not modified after this fixture failure.
2. `package_fix1.py` initially had an unescaped apostrophe in a generated English string. Python failed to parse it before staging. Exact source and syntax traceback are in `logs/failures/package_script_v1/`; the string was fixed, staging then succeeded. No candidate native changed.
3. A tool session launch returned `StreamingExecNotEnabledContainerError`; no process was started. Work continued using bounded blocking commands. One long unit-test tool transport timed out while the underlying command completed and produced the negative-fixture failure. Process/log inspection confirmed completion; the failure was retained, not claimed as a passed or running test.
4. The inherited diagnostic price quotes are own-only quotations. Even whole-step cash equality is not proof of uniquely known unseen rival orders. Confirmed own fertilizer quantity arrivals and actual whole-step cash deltas are stored separately. Do not sum non-reconciling quoted prices and call them actual purchase spending.
5. No saved post-divergence future is used as a candidate's real future. Historical action probes stop at their first divergence. The 10 new terminal forks use official generated observations and explicitly synthetic opponent private inventory/actions. They are 470 bounded transitions and zero complete games. They are not the original R2 rematch or a new win rate.
6. Three recorded r3 histories retain all719 actions; that is preservation on those trajectories, not a fresh win count. The target recorded loss has not been rerun against its real opponent. Exact opponent-pool testing and the >85% strict-win target remain unverified.
7. Only the terminal cash-admission issue is changed. Earlier quantity/forecast opportunity costs remain. The existing public-flow model approximates market and labor behavior. Two scenarios are not exhaustive, and the 48-transition bound adds runtime near the end. No worst-case latency guarantee is claimed.
8. Historical parent r2 1087/1536, central r3 case outcomes, author's old r3 tests, and current fix1 conditional results must not be pooled into one denominator. Frozen parent full archive is preserved unchanged.
